# Meeting At 10 v2 rebuild

Status: in progress on branch `redesign/v2`. Master: this delta.

Rebuild the team site from a scaffold, without `@meetingat10/design-system` or Tailwind. Keep the content, anchors, build-time URLs and static export. Only the visual language and the components change.

## Direction

The page is one working day. Eleven students and one standing meeting make twelve marks on a clock: each surname holds an hour and the 10 o'clock mark is the meeting. The hero clock docks in the top bar and scrolling moves it from 10:00 to 17:00, while the sky behind the page changes with the hour.

- Ground: 15 minute color bands from morning sky `#8ED2FF` through noon, peach and sunset `#FF7A45` to night `#031B38`. The page has this one theme and does not follow the OS color scheme, because the day is the identity.
- Accent: Sun `#FFD21E`, the clock face and the lunch band. Appointment blue `#005FDB` for calendar blocks, taken from the logo. Navy `#031B38` for ink.
- Type: Funnel Display for headlines and names, Funnel Sans for reading, Martian Mono for times.
- Signature: the clock face. Hovering or focusing a name swings the hour hand to that person.

## Sections

Hero clock, standup and day sheet (`#about`), roster (`#team`), build blocks (`#projects`), lunch, review with module index and request path (`#architecture`), process (`#timeline`), numbers (`#stats`), ship links (`#contact`), night footer.
